## Disease Distribution

- The dataset contains 15 disease categories including "No Finding".
- The dataset is highly imbalanced.
- "No Finding" is the largest class with 60,361 images.
- Hernia is the rarest disease with only 227 images.
- Class imbalance indicates that evaluation should emphasize Recall, Precision, F1-score, and ROC-AUC instead of Accuracy alone.
